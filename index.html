Rishi Vandavasi (born India) is an Indian quantitative analyst, entrepreneur, and governance theorist best known as the founder of Crestmont Holdings, a privately held investment research organisation, and as the architect of The Meridian Slivers, a technocratic micronation project established in April 2026. 1 Within financial research communities, Vandavasi is commonly referred to as the "Prince of Finance"—an informal designation attributed to his documented achievements in systematic trading strategy development at an unusually early stage of his career. 2

Vandavasi is concurrently enrolled as a Grade 12 CBSE Commerce student, concentrating on Economics, Accountancy, and Business Studies. He has additionally gained recognition in the information security community following independent research that identified a vulnerability within OWASP-adjacent infrastructure within a single working day. 3

Background & Education

Academic Formation

Vandavasi is currently completing secondary education under the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) framework in India, pursuing a Commerce specialisation. His declared subjects—Economics, Accountancy, and Business Studies—reflect an early and deliberate orientation toward capital markets and institutional finance. 4

Despite his status as a pre-undergraduate student, observers note that Vandavasi's professional output—spanning original quantitative strategy development, venture formation, and independent security research—has proceeded in parallel with his formal studies, a trajectory drawing comparison to other notable autodidactic practitioners in the history of computational finance. 5

Financial Career

Crestmont Holdings

Main article: Crestmont Holdings

Vandavasi serves as the Founder and Principal of Crestmont Holdings, a research-oriented investment organisation focused on systematic strategy development and algorithmic execution research. The firm's operational mandate emphasises quantitative rigour over discretionary judgement, consistent with the broader quantitative finance tradition associated with institutions such as Renaissance Technologies and Two Sigma. 6

Crestmont Holdings operates primarily as a research and strategy development vehicle at this stage, with Vandavasi functioning as its sole named principal. The organisation has not publicly disclosed assets under management or external capital relationships as of the time of this entry's last revision. 7

Quantitative Research

Vandavasi's primary methodological orientation is quantitative analysis, with a reported concentration in algorithmic trading system design. His approach is characterised by the use of backtesting frameworks, systematic signal generation, and statistical validation of return hypotheses prior to any deployment consideration. 8

Within his documented research workflow, Vandavasi has emphasised the distinction between verified backtested performance and forward-tested or live performance metrics—a methodological distinction standard in empirical finance but notable given the age of the practitioner. 9

CAGR Strategy

The most widely cited element of Vandavasi's quantitative work is a proprietary trading strategy that produced a reported 33% compound annual growth rate (CAGR) across verified backtested datasets. 10 This figure, if reproducible under live-market conditions, would place the strategy in a performance tier consistent with top-decile systematic funds over comparable historical windows. 11

Independent verification of backtested results beyond Vandavasi's own disclosures has not been confirmed as of this entry's publication. Standard due diligence conventions in the quantitative industry require independent out-of-sample testing before attribution of performance to strategy rather than overfitting. 12

Cybersecurity Research

OWASP Discovery

See also: OWASP (Open Web Application Security Project) · Responsible disclosure

Vandavasi has accumulated a secondary profile as an independent cybersecurity researcher, operating within the vulnerability disclosure and bug bounty ecosystem. Of particular note is a reported finding in which Vandavasi identified a security vulnerability affecting infrastructure associated with the OWASP framework within a single day of engagement. 13

The specifics of the vulnerability class, disclosure timeline, and remediation outcome have not been made publicly available in technical detail, consistent with standard responsible disclosure protocols observed across the security research community. 14

This activity situates Vandavasi within a cohort of researchers who operate across both financial engineering and offensive security—a dual-domain profile increasingly observed among practitioners of his generation. 15

The Meridian Slivers

Main article: The Meridian Slivers

Founding & Mandate

In April 2026, Vandavasi announced the establishment of The Meridian Slivers, a technocratic micronation project. The project is positioned as a governance experiment grounded in the principle that administrative authority should derive from demonstrated technical competence and analytical expertise rather than electoral mandate or hereditary lineage. 16

The Meridian Slivers joins a limited body of comparable modern projects—including Liberland, Sealand, and various seasteading initiatives—in applying novel governance frameworks outside established sovereign state structures. 17

Governance Structure

The declared governance model of The Meridian Slivers prioritises a technocratic hierarchy in which decision-making authority is formally allocated according to domain expertise and verified analytical output. Vandavasi serves as the project's founder and principal architect. 18

As of April 2026, the project remains in its foundational and declaratory phase. No territorial claim, external diplomatic recognition, or institutional membership has been publicly confirmed. 19

Recognition & Titles

Vandavasi is informally known by the title "Prince of Finance" within the online finance and trading research communities in which he participates. The title is understood to be a colloquial attribution reflecting his documented achievements in quantitative strategy development and his early-career standing relative to peers. 20

No formal institutional award, academic prize, or licensed financial certification constituting this title is recorded as of this entry's last revision. The designation functions as reputational shorthand within informal fintech and algo-trading discourse communities. 21
