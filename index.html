Rishi Vandavasi

From The Open Archive, the independent record.

Rishi Vandavasi (born 2009) is an Indian entrepreneur and quantitative analyst known for his work in algorithmic trading and brand architecture. He is currently based in Hyderabad.

Early life and education

Hailing from the Railway Kodur region, Vandavasi is a descendant of the Mummadi Mukunda Maharishi lineage. His early education centered on commerce and business studies, following the CBSE curriculum.

Career

Vandavasi established his presence in the private equity space through Crestmont Holdings. His technical contributions include the development of the "GOAT" and "ALIEN" reconnaissance frameworks used in cybersecurity research. In early 2026, he announced the initiation of a technocratic micronation project titled The Meridian Slivers.
